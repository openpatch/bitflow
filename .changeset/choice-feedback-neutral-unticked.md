The choice task no longer badges a wrong answer the learner *correctly left unticked* as green "correct" — that told students a wrong answer was the right one. Outcome markers now say what happened to each choice: a ticked correct choice is green "correct", a ticked wrong choice is red "wrong", a correct choice the learner failed to tick is shown green with a dashed border as "correct answer, not selected", and a wrong choice left unticked carries no marker at all. Scoring, including partial credit, is unchanged.
